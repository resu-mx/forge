# Software Engineer I (New Grad)

**Brightline Logistics** - Columbus, OH (hybrid)

Brightline moves freight for manufacturers across the Midwest. The operations tooling team
builds the internal services that dispatchers and planners rely on all day.

## In this role you will

- Build and maintain Python services, mostly Flask, backed by PostgreSQL.
- Improve the dashboards and alerts that track shipments in transit.
- Package services with Docker and help move them onto shared infrastructure.
- Pair with senior engineers and take part in code review from your first week.

## Who we are looking for

- Recent graduates with a degree in computer science or a related field.
- Former Brightline interns are encouraged to apply.
- Comfort with SQL and at least one backend language.

Salary range: $76,000 - $84,000, plus a signing bonus for new graduates.
