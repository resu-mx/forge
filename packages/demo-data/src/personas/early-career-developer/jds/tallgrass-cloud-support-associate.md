# Cloud Support Associate

**Tallgrass Software** - Remote (US)

Tallgrass hosts its clinic scheduling product for thousands of customers. Cloud support
associates are the first technical responders when something goes wrong.

## Responsibilities

- Investigate customer reports of slow pages, failed logins and sync errors.
- Read logs on Linux hosts and in our container platform to find the cause.
- Diagnose DNS, TLS and HTTP issues between clinics and our service.
- Write clear incident notes and improve the support knowledge base.

## Qualifications

- Experience in a help desk, IT support or similar customer-facing technical role.
- Networking fundamentals and comfort on the Linux command line.
- Familiarity with Docker is a plus.

Salary range: $62,000 - $70,000, remote within the US with occasional evening coverage.
