# Associate Platform Engineer

**Kestrel Analytics** - Cincinnati, OH (hybrid, three days in office)

Kestrel Analytics builds forecasting dashboards for regional retailers. Our platform team keeps
the build, deploy and monitoring tooling healthy for about forty engineers.

## What you will do

- Maintain container images and the CI pipelines that build them.
- Write small services and scripts in Python to automate routine operations work.
- Help migrate batch jobs onto our Kubernetes cluster.
- Improve dashboards and alerts so problems are caught before customers notice.
- Join a light, well-supported on-call rotation after your first three months.

## What we are looking for

- A degree in computer science or equivalent practical experience.
- Comfort on the Linux command line and with Docker.
- Some exposure to infrastructure as code (Terraform or similar).
- Clear written communication; we review each other's changes carefully.

## Nice to have

- Experience with Grafana or another metrics stack.
- A project you can walk us through end to end.

Salary range: $72,000 - $86,000, plus an annual learning budget.
