# Associate Solutions Engineer

**Harborview Health Partners** - Cleveland, OH (hybrid)

Our digital services group builds the scheduling and referral tools that clinics rely on.
Solutions engineers sit between clinical staff and the development team: they
gather requirements, prototype integrations and help roll changes out.

## Responsibilities

- Meet with clinic managers to understand workflow problems and document them clearly.
- Build small integrations against our internal REST APIs and write the SQL behind reports.
- Prepare demos and short training guides for new features.
- Triage support requests that reach the engineering team and turn them into tickets.

## Qualifications

- One to two years of experience in software, IT support or a related field, including
  internships.
- Working knowledge of JavaScript and SQL.
- Patience and clarity when explaining technical trade-offs to non-technical colleagues.

## Benefits

Health, dental and vision coverage from your first week, tuition assistance, and a hybrid schedule.

Salary range: $70,000 - $82,000.
