# Zero Trust Architect

**Halyard Analytics Group** - Arlington, VA (hybrid, two days on site)

Halyard's Zero Trust Practice helps defense customers replace perimeter-based networks with
identity-aware access. We are a small, senior team that designs the target architecture and
then stays to make sure it is actually deployed.

## Responsibilities

- Assess customer environments and write zero trust target architectures and roadmaps.
- Design identity, device posture and segmentation controls with customer engineering teams.
- Lead pilots end to end and measure their effect on lateral movement and user friction.
- Brief program leadership and translate their priorities into engineering plans.

## Requirements

- Active Top Secret clearance; SCI eligibility strongly preferred.
- Eight or more years in network or security engineering.
- Hands-on experience with SAML or OIDC federation, microsegmentation and least-privilege
  access design.
- Comfort presenting to senior government and military stakeholders.

## Nice to have

- A completed zero trust pilot you can walk us through, including what did not work.

Salary range: $192,000 - $220,000, with a 10% annual bonus target.
