# Security Engineering Manager

**Capitol Beacon Consulting** - Washington, DC (hybrid)

Capitol Beacon is a boutique firm advising federal agencies on security modernization. We are
hiring a manager to lead a team of eight engineers split across three agency engagements.

## Responsibilities

- Hire, coach and grow a team of security engineers and assessors.
- Set technical direction across engagements and review key deliverables.
- Own client relationships with agency CISOs and program managers.
- Contribute to proposals and capture efforts for new work.

## Requirements

- Active Top Secret clearance.
- Ten or more years in security engineering, including at least two managing people.
- Experience leading authorization and continuous monitoring programs.
- Strong presentation and writing skills.

## Nice to have

- Experience mentoring engineers who are transitioning from military service.

Salary range: $196,000 - $232,000, plus a performance bonus.
