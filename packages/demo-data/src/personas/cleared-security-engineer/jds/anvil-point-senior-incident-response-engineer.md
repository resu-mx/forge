# Senior Incident Response Engineer

**Anvil Point Cyber** - Aurora, CO (hybrid; on-call rotation one week in five)

Anvil Point provides managed detection and response to defense suppliers. When a customer is
breached, our responders are the ones on the bridge call.

## What you will do

- Lead investigations from first alert through containment, eradication and recovery.
- Collect and analyze endpoint and memory evidence with osquery and our forensic tooling.
- Write after-action reports that customer executives can act on.
- Automate the repetitive parts of investigations in Python and PowerShell.
- Coach mid-level responders during live incidents.

## What we need

- Five or more years of hands-on incident response, ideally including tier-3 work.
- Strong Windows and Linux forensics fundamentals.
- An active Secret clearance or higher.
- A calm voice on a bad day.

## Nice to have

- An incident handling certification from a recognized body.
- Prior experience on a defense security operations contract.

Salary range: $170,000 - $198,000, plus an on-call stipend.
