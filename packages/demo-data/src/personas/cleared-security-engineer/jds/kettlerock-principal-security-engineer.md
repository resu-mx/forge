# Principal Security Engineer

**Kettlerock Mission Systems** - Annapolis Junction, MD (on site, four days a week)

Kettlerock builds and operates mission data platforms for intelligence community customers.
Our security engineering group designs the controls that let analysts move fast on
classified networks without widening the attack surface.

## What you will do

- Own the security architecture for a multi-enclave container platform, from identity and
  segmentation to signed software supply chains.
- Design detection coverage with the security operations team and measure how well it works.
- Lead threat modeling sessions for new mission capabilities before they are built.
- Run the internal certificate authority roadmap and retire long-lived credentials.
- Mentor senior engineers and review their designs.

## Required

- Active TS/SCI with CI polygraph. This position cannot be filled without it.
- Ten or more years in security engineering, including at least three as a technical lead.
- Deep experience with Kubernetes security, PKI and zero trust access patterns.
- Scripting in Python or Go.

## Preferred

- A senior security engineering certification such as CSSE or equivalent.
- Experience writing detection content for network and endpoint telemetry.

Salary range: $190,000 - $228,000, plus a clearance bonus and on-site parking.
