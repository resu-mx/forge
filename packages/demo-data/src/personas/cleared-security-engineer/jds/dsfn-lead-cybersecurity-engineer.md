# Lead Cybersecurity Engineer

**Directorate of Secure Federal Networks** - Washington, DC (on site, telework eligible two days each pay period)

The Directorate of Secure Federal Networks operates the shared backbone and identity services
used by a dozen civilian agencies. The Office of Engineering is hiring a lead engineer to
shape how those systems are authorized and defended over the next five years.

## Duties

- Serve as the technical lead for authorization of enterprise network and identity systems,
  working with system owners, assessors and the authorizing official.
- Define the directorate's continuous monitoring strategy and the automation behind it.
- Guide the zero trust architecture program across segmentation, identity and device trust.
- Review vulnerability and configuration findings and set remediation priorities.
- Write and present technical decision papers for senior leadership.

## Qualifications

- Applicants must be U.S. citizens and able to obtain and maintain a Top Secret clearance
  with SCI eligibility. Current clearance holders are preferred.
- Specialized experience equivalent to the next lower grade in the federal service,
  including leading security control assessments and authorization packages.
- Demonstrated ability to write clearly for technical and non-technical readers.

## How to apply

Submit a federal-format resume listing weekly hours and a supervisor contact for each
position, plus a transcript if claiming education. Veterans' preference applies.

Salary range: $163,000 - $191,000 a year, locality pay included.
