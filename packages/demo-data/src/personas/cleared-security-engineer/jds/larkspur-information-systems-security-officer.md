# Information Systems Security Officer

**Larkspur Integrated Systems** - Washington, DC (on site at the customer facility)

Larkspur supports a civilian agency's case management systems. The ISSO keeps three systems
authorized and is the day-to-day security point of contact for the government system owner.

## Duties

- Maintain system security plans, contingency plans and plans of action and milestones.
- Coordinate annual control assessments and track remediation to closure.
- Review monthly vulnerability scan results and configuration compliance reports.
- Process access requests and conduct quarterly account reviews.
- Report security incidents to the agency within required timelines.

## Requirements

- Active Top Secret clearance.
- Five or more years supporting authorization under a risk management framework.
- Excellent written communication and attention to detail.

## Preferred

- An authorization or assessment practitioner certification.

Salary range: $150,000 - $172,000.
