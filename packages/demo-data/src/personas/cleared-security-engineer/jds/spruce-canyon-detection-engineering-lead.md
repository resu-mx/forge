# Detection Engineering Lead

**Spruce Canyon Technologies** - Colorado Springs, CO (on site)

Spruce Canyon supports space and missile defense programs with secure ground systems. Our
cyber defense team is growing, and we need a lead to build the detection engineering function
from a handful of analysts' scripts into a real practice.

## Responsibilities

- Build and lead a team of four detection engineers.
- Own the detection rule lifecycle: hypothesis, testing against replayed data, deployment and
  retirement.
- Expand network sensor coverage with Zeek and Suricata across classified and unclassified
  enclaves.
- Partner with incident responders to close gaps found during investigations.

## Requirements

- Active TS/SCI.
- Six or more years in security operations or detection engineering, with at least two
  leading others.
- Experience tuning SIEM correlation rules and network intrusion detection signatures.

## Preferred

- An incident handling certification.
- Experience building a detection test lab.

Salary range: $176,000 - $204,000, with relocation assistance available.
