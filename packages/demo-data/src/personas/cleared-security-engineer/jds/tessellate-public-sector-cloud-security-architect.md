# Public Sector Cloud Security Architect

**Tessellate Cloud** - Remote (US); travel to customer sites in the Washington, DC area up to 30%

Tessellate Cloud runs isolated regions for government customers. Public sector security
architects help agencies and their integrators design workloads that pass authorization the
first time.

## What you will do

- Lead security architecture reviews for agency workloads moving onto Tessellate's
  government regions.
- Build reference architectures for Kubernetes, key management and private networking.
- Turn recurring customer findings into product feedback for our platform teams.
- Present at customer briefings and industry events.

## What you bring

- Seven or more years in security engineering or architecture, with time on a federal program.
- Strong background in container platforms, PKI and infrastructure as code (Terraform or similar).
- An active Secret clearance or higher, or the ability to obtain one.
- The judgment to say no to a design, and the patience to explain why.

## Nice to have

- A Tessellate Cloud Security Specialty, or willingness to earn it in your first six months.

Salary range: $205,000 - $245,000 base, plus equity and an annual bonus.
