# Principal Robot Learning Engineer

**Ironwood Robotics** - San Jose, CA

Ironwood's picking robots work in more than sixty warehouses. We want them to learn new
items and new bins from a handful of examples instead of weeks of tuning, and we are hiring a
principal engineer to lead that effort.

## What you will do

- Set the technical direction for learned grasping and manipulation across the fleet.
- Build training pipelines that learn from fleet data and a small number of demonstrations.
- Close the loop between simulation, training and field rollout.
- Mentor the perception and motion teams.

## What we need

- Deep experience in robot learning, ideally including reinforcement or imitation learning.
- Production C++ and Python; ROS 2 experience.
- Computer vision for manipulation, and training models in PyTorch.
- A Ph.D. or equivalent research experience.

Salary range: $235,000 - $280,000, plus equity.
