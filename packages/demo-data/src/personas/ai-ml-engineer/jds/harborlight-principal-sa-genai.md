# Principal Solutions Architect, Generative AI

**Harborlight Cloud** - Bellevue, WA (hybrid)

Harborlight Cloud's generative AI field team helps our largest customers move from prototypes
to production. As a principal solutions architect you will be the senior technical voice in the
room for customers building assistants, retrieval systems and agents on our platform.

## In this role you will

- Lead architecture engagements for strategic accounts, from discovery to production launch.
- Design reference architectures for retrieval-augmented generation and agent workloads.
- Write the blueprints, sample code and guidance other architects reuse.
- Represent customer needs to our product and service teams.
- Speak at customer events and internal enablement sessions.

## Basic qualifications

- Ten or more years in software engineering, ML engineering or solutions architecture.
- Hands-on experience shipping LLM-backed applications to production.
- Depth in distributed systems and container orchestration.

## Preferred qualifications

- Advanced degree in a technical field.
- A professional-level Harborlight Cloud certification, or the ability to earn one within six
  months.

Salary range: $230,000 - $275,000, plus stock and a sign-on bonus.
