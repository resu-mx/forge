# Staff ML Platform Engineer

**Northbeam Logistics** - Remote (US)

Northbeam runs freight networks across North America, and about forty data scientists build
the routing, pricing and forecasting models behind them. The ML platform team gives them a
paved road from notebook to production.

## Responsibilities

- Own the training and serving platform: scheduling, experiment tracking and model registry.
- Run distributed training on Kubernetes and make GPU capacity easy to share.
- Define the standards for model deployment, monitoring and rollback.
- Lead design reviews and mentor engineers across the platform group.

## Qualifications

- Eight or more years in software or ML infrastructure engineering.
- Production experience with Kubernetes, containers and a distributed compute framework.
- Experience with experiment tracking and MLOps practices at scale.
- Strong Python.

Salary range: $210,000 - $250,000, plus annual bonus.
