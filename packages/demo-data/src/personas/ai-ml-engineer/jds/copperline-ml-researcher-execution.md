# Machine Learning Researcher, Execution

**Copperline Markets** - New York, NY (hybrid)

Copperline is a small market-making firm. The execution research group studies how to place
and size orders with the least market impact, and increasingly uses reinforcement learning to
do it. Researchers here own an idea from first experiment to production trading.

## What you will do

- Formulate execution problems as sequential decision tasks and build simulators for them.
- Train and evaluate policies with careful experimental design and honest baselines.
- Publish internally, review peers' research and present results to the trading desk.
- Partner with engineers to deploy policies safely with tight risk limits.

## What we look for

- A Ph.D. in machine learning, robotics, operations research or a related field, or
  equivalent research experience.
- A record of publications or talks in reinforcement learning or a neighboring area.
- Fluency in Python and one of JAX or PyTorch; NumPy is second nature.

Please include a CV with publications and presentations.
Compensation: $220,000 - $300,000 base, plus bonus.
