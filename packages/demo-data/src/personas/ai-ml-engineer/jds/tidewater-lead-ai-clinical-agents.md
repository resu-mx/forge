# Lead AI Engineer, Clinical Agents

**Tidewater Health Systems** - Sacramento, CA (on-site, five days a week)

Tidewater operates twelve hospitals and a network of clinics. Our digital health group is
building assistants that draft discharge summaries, answer staff policy questions and route
patient messages, always with a clinician in the loop.

## What you will do

- Lead a team of four engineers building retrieval-augmented assistants and agent workflows.
- Design guardrails, audit logging and human review for every clinical-facing feature.
- Work with compliance and clinical leadership on safe rollout plans.

## Requirements

- Seven or more years in software or ML engineering, including LLM-backed products.
- Experience designing defenses against prompt injection and data leakage.
- Willingness to work on site at our Sacramento campus.

Salary range: $200,000 - $240,000, plus a retention bonus.
