# Solutions Architect, AI Partners

**Harborlight Cloud** - Remote (US)

The AI partners team works with software companies that build agent and model tooling on top
of Harborlight Cloud. You will help partners integrate cleanly, publish joint reference
designs and launch listings in the Harborlight catalog.

## What you will do

- Guide partner engineering teams through integration and launch reviews.
- Build example integrations, including tool servers that follow open agent protocols.
- Co-write technical guides and run partner enablement workshops.

## What you bring

- Six or more years in software engineering or solutions architecture.
- Practical experience with large language models and agent tool interfaces.
- Strong technical writing; you enjoy explaining how things fit together.

Up to 30% travel. Salary range: $190,000 - $230,000.
