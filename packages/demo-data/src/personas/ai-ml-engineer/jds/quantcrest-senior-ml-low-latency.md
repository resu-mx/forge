# Senior ML Engineer, Low-Latency Signals

**Quantcrest Capital** - New York, NY (on-site)

Quantcrest is a systematic trading firm. The signals team builds the models and the serving
path that turn market data into trading decisions in microseconds. We are hiring an engineer
who is equally comfortable training a model and profiling the code that serves it.

## The work

- Train and validate short-horizon predictive models on high-frequency market data.
- Move models from research into the production serving path in C++.
- Profile and remove latency from feature computation and inference.
- Build streaming feature pipelines and the monitoring around them.

## About you

- Strong C++ and Python; you have shipped latency-sensitive systems.
- Solid grounding in statistics and machine learning; PyTorch experience preferred.
- Comfortable on Linux, with experience in event streaming.
- An advanced degree in a quantitative field is a plus, not a requirement.

Base salary: $250,000 - $325,000, plus a discretionary bonus.
